## reserve available stock
reserved 2 of STAPLER for alice (id=R1001)
count STAPLER onHand=4 reserved=2 available=2

## reject reservation when stock is unavailable
cannot reserve 5 of STAPLER for bob: insufficient stock

## confirm reservation ships an order
reserved 2 of STAPLER for alice (id=R1001)
reservation R1001 confirmed and shipped as O1002
count STAPLER onHand=2 reserved=0 available=2

## release reservation returns stock to availability
reserved 2 of STAPLER for alice (id=R1001)
reservation R1001 released
count STAPLER onHand=4 reserved=0 available=4

## reservation expires before the next command
reserved 2 of STAPLER for alice (id=R1001)
reservation R1001 expired
count STAPLER onHand=4 reserved=0 available=4

## expired reservation cannot be confirmed
reserved 2 of STAPLER for alice (id=R1001)
reservation R1001 expired
cannot confirm R1001: reservation expired or not found
